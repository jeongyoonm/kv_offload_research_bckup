Packed storage is opt-in and exact at predictor frames/state ownership; do not enable by default based on these results. No serving change was made to original parent workspace.
The fundamental remaining bottleneck is scheduling cadence during load-only periods. Compute duration prediction is absent then, so improving its MAE cannot resolve waits for the fixed ready-load target. A readiness/throughput-value model or controlled initial partial-load dispatch should be evaluated separately, with fixed seeds and enough repeats to distinguish cadence regime changes. The present productive policy had an observed activation in yc4, but no consistent serving benefit, and zero activations in yc5 combined cells. No untested mechanism is promoted.
