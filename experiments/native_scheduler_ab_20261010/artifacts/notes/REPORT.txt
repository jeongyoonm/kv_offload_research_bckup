Interpretation and sensitivity
------------------------------
The earlier claim of substantially smaller 60K scheduler gain is not
reliably reproduced here: full paired gains are 3.92% and 3.79%.
60K pairs 0-2 gain 1.35-2.52%; pair 3 gains 9.44%. Keep all pairs.
The final C60K episode had 1002.45 ms of inter-compute gaps vs 90.14,
126.06 and 116.28 ms previously. Its execution envelopes were shorter,
8955.14 ms vs 9254.95-9310.40 ms; the slowdown is outside them.
Only 2/9 transitions dispatched B before prior compute ended (1/9
submitted forward before it ended), versus 13/14 and 12-13/14 in
earlier C60K cells. D60K retained 10-11/11 early dispatches.

The final C60K gaps partition into 205.99 ms before scheduling,
189.91 ms scheduler, 273.84 ms dispatch-to-worker, 195.79 ms request
state, plus later phases. All ranks show similar delays in the largest
gaps; this is not evidence of one slow GPU rank or a slower kernel.
Measured markers do not identify a deeper OS/GC/serialization root
cause. The trace establishes loss of preparation/submission overlap.

For the first three 60K pairs, compute rounds fall 15 to 12 but
envelope savings are only about 1.7%. More batching does not imply
equally large elapsed-time savings. These long-prefill envelopes
already occupy most of the serving interval. This is consistent with
amortizing batch overhead; kernel occupancy was not separately measured.

16K averaged 11.75 to 11.25 rounds, 2685.65 to 2577.49 ms envelopes,
and 223.27 to 173.91 ms gaps. Individual pairs differ: one improves
gaps without fewer rounds; another saves envelopes while adding gaps.
The aggregate is a preparation/sealing package effect, not an isolated
hold/deadline benefit. Total uncached token work is identical across arms.

Admissions and remaining waiting
--------------------------------
short16k: 43.50 admissions/episode; 34.25/64.50 opportunities; pooled recovery 53.10%; hold outside compute 131.39 ms.
familiar60k: 66.25 admissions/episode; 66.00/73.75 opportunities; pooled recovery 89.49%; hold outside compute 0.40 ms.
Opportunity denominator: requests whose all-rank KV completion falls
inside any measured compute interval; numerator intersects these with
admitted requests. Different arms have different timing windows.
Hold outside compute is base-ready-to-seal time outside envelopes,
including load-only waiting, not an additive throughput cost.

Store flush-wait and reuse-fence exposed time were zero in audited
metadata-to-forward windows in both arms. Short D still exposed about
13.11 ms/episode of store descriptor construction in those windows.
Store diagnostics overlap pre-forward phases; do not add them twice.
Zero in this window is not a claim of no stores or zero transfer time.

Validation and limitations
--------------------------
All 16 serving cells accepted: full CPU prefix restoration, no GPU
prefix hits/preemptions, one output token, frozen estimator, C zero late
admissions/compact/pinned work, D preparation observed on all 8 ranks.
Engine execution envelopes and all gap/client partitions checked.
Execution envelopes include submission and collective waiting, not pure
useful GPU kernel time. Four pairs do not establish significance.
No model numerical-equivalence claim; original sparse selector retained.

Initial readiness tests: 593 passed, 22 skipped, 3 failed; same three
failed in unchanged parent. Fixture/dependency repairs: 3 targeted passed.
See GATE_NOTES.txt and preserved original logs. No serving code changed.
